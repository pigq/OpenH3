Source offers and unresolved rights
-----------------------------------
GPL components require a corresponding-source offer appropriate to the final
distribution channel. OpenH3 does not claim that offer is complete in this
preview. H3 weights, derived LoRAs and converted checkpoints are not bundled.
The embedded workflow graphs are distinct from those model files and retain
their upstream terms, including applicable territorial restrictions. No
standalone 7-Zip or Bun executable is included in the application runtime.
